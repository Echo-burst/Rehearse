Rehearse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Rehearse is a student project that lets you practise difficult conversations with an AI. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address from Google.
  • What you create in the app: the conversations you set up, your rehearsal messages, scores and feedback, your energy balance, your colour settings, and any review you choose to send.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your conversations and progress.
  • To generate the other person's replies, hints and feedback: your rehearsal messages are sent to an AI service (Groq) only to create the answer.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your information for marketing.

Who can see your data

  • Only you can see your conversations and rehearsals. This is enforced by the database itself.
  • The app administrator can see general numbers (for example, how many rehearsals an account has and its average score), but not your topics or messages.
  • If you send a review, the administrator sees it together with your name and email.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The app is hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open your profile and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything you created.
